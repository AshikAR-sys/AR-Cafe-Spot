The house

A room built for lingering.

AR CAFE SPOT began with one espresso machine, a borrowed grinder and a stubborn idea: that a neighbourhood deserves a place to sit still. The room is deliberately quiet — soft light, brass, stone, and music kept low enough to talk over.

We buy from small roasters who know their farmers by name, and we pull every espresso to order. Nothing is held under a lamp. If a pastry is gone by eleven, it was baked at five.

Everything is made the slow way, because the slow way tastes better.
