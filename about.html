Our story

A room, a grinder, an idea

AR CAFE SPOT is small on purpose. We would rather do a few things properly than a lot of things quickly.

How it started

From a market stall to Marlowe Lane

We started in 2019 with a two-group machine on a Saturday market stall, selling eighty cups before noon and running out of milk twice. The queue was the whole business plan.

Two years later we found the room on Marlowe Lane — an old print shop with tall windows and a stone floor. We kept the floor, added a marble counter, and spent far too long choosing the light fittings.

What has not changed is the way we work: beans from roasters we can ring up, milk from a farm an hour away, and pastries made in our own kitchen before the sun is properly up.

What we care about

Three small promises

01

Fresh, or not at all

Nothing sits under a lamp. Coffee is pulled when you order it, food is made when you ask for it, and pastries that don’t sell are given away at closing.

02

People we can name

Our roaster, our dairy, our baker and the grower behind this month’s single origin. If we can’t tell you where something came from, we don’t serve it.

03

A quiet kind of hospitality

No rush, no upselling, no music you have to shout over. Come for ten minutes or three hours — both are welcome here.

Our favourite review

“I came in for a coffee and stayed for the afternoon. Nobody minded.”

— a regular

The kitchen

Baked here, every morning

Our pastry bench starts at five. Croissants are laminated over three days, buns are proved slowly overnight, and the cheesecake is deliberately underbaked in the middle.

The menu follows the market: stone fruit in summer, quince and pear in autumn, blood orange through the winter. If something looks different from last week, that is usually the point.
